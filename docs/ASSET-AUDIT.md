## Creative direction

Near-black editorial canvas; Syne display type and DM Sans UI; cyan as the principal accent, indigo and magenta confined to timeline details. The section sequence moves from footage to collection, personal introduction, toolkit, experience, education, process and contact. Motion uses short opacity/transform reveals, a restrained particle network and editing-inspired timelines. Video interaction takes priority over decoration.

## Delivery decisions

- Buildless HTML, CSS and ES modules; no client framework or runtime dependency.
- H.264 / yuv420p MP4 with AAC audio and fast-start metadata for browser compatibility. CRF 21; width capped at 1080 pixels; source frame rates retained. Tall frames are never stretched. Larger logo studies are downscaled for web delivery.
- WebP posters are extracted from actual video frames. No substitute stock images or artificial portrait are used.
- Only the hero video may load automatically. The featured player and gallery videos load on intentional play/hover. At most one card preview is active; previews pause and release their sources when out of view.
- Reduced motion and Save-Data disable hero autoplay and card previews. All explicit playback remains available.
- Local preview supports HTTP byte ranges for seeking.
